# 2026-09-06

_Internal session log. Append-only._

One long session, five pieces of work. Everything below was verified in a browser or by test, not
by inspection alone.

## 1. Events page redesign, and the Past Events reel

Rebuilt `/events` around a new signature component. `PastEventsReel.vue` renders the archive as a
film strip on a rotated plane with perspective, looping seamlessly by carrying two identical halves
and sliding exactly 50%. Hover, focus or tap holds the reel and lifts the frame forward, undoing
the plane's rotation so the held frame squares up while the rest stays in the strip.

Also extracted `src/data/events.js` (previously inline arrays in the view), rebuilt the hero,
highlight, upcoming grid and host CTA, and dropped a hardcoded fake featured event with a fake
countdown that was not in any dataset.

Em dashes in eleven archive titles were converted to colons at the owner's content rule. No event
information was lost.

## 2. The reel on the homepage too

Moved the reel's whole implementation into itself: `src/assets/reel.css` is imported by the
component, so any page that mounts it gets the geometry, loop, hover and reduced-motion fallback.
A `variant` prop swaps four geometry custom properties: `archive` on `/events`, `compact` on the
homepage. Added an IntersectionObserver that pauses the strip when it is more than a screen away,
since the homepage now carries one below the fold.

Placed as "From the House" between the offerings grid and the academic CTA, which moved the
academic band one step along the two-tone rhythm rather than putting two forest bands together.

## 3. Document library, then reader, then index

Built in three passes as the brief evolved:

1. A library with a category rail, search, list and detail panel
2. Added an in-site reader at `/documents/:id?` with a viewer component and five strategies
3. Collapsed the whole thing to index -> reader when the middle step proved redundant for eleven
   documents

Ended at: a three-column card index where each card is one link, and a reader that embeds the
document with an "In this archive" disclosure for switching without going back.

**Embed verification** (the useful artifact): checked every supplied URL's headers and watched each
render. Nine embed, two cannot, each for a stated reason. Recorded in `docs/decisions.md`.

Fixed a real layout bug on the way: the detail panel was `position: sticky`, and a pinned panel
taller than the viewport puts its own actions permanently out of reach.

## 4. Nav rail hover, and nav order

Reordered the rail to Home, Study Corner, Documents, Events, Meetups, Leaderboard, Community,
Teams, About, Contact. Only About actually moved.

Toned down the hover: the gaussian field went from `maxOffset 8 / sigma 0.9` to `4 / 0.5`. Measured
live before and after; neighbours went from 4.32px of travel to 0.54px.

## 5. Dark theme lock, responsiveness audit, code hygiene

Audited first, then changed only what the evidence showed.

- **Responsiveness: nothing to fix.** Swept 11 routes at 375/390/430/768/1024/1280/1440/1920/2560/
  3840, scrolled to the bottom of each, looked for any element escaping the viewport outside a
  deliberately-scrolling container. Zero overflow everywhere.
- **Contrast: one real defect.** `--color-cream-faint` failed WCAG AA at 3.2 to 3.5:1. Lifted to
  `#8e7e5d`. A 12-route audit now reports zero elements below AA.
- **Theme lock added**: `src/assets/theme-lock.css` plus three head metas. 40 selectors were
  painting a background while inheriting their text colour; the 20 text-bearing ones now restate it.
- **One broken link**: footer Privacy and Terms were `href="#"`, which under hash routing threw the
  reader to the homepage. Removed.
- **Not ours**: `DOCS_timing is not defined` on the reader is Google's script inside their frame.
  Proved by loading the identical shell without an iframe.
